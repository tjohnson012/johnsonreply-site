FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The tests

Am I locked in?

No. The tests are plain open-source Playwright in your own repo. If we stop working together, you keep every test and they keep running.

Can you work with tests we already have?

Yes. I can extend an existing Playwright suite, fix a flaky one, or migrate from Cypress or Selenium.

What do you need from us?

A staging URL, test accounts and access to the repo. I never need production data.

What can you test?

Any web app, whatever it's built with: Chrome, Safari, Firefox and mobile browsers, plus API checks alongside the UI tests.

Pricing

What's the difference between the two plans?

Jump Start is a one-time build that your team runs and maintains. With Coverage-as-a-Service, I run, maintain and extend the suite every month and investigate every failure.

Do I pay for test creation on the monthly plan?

No. Building the initial suite is included, with no setup fee.

We're hiring a QA engineer. Why do this?

Hiring takes months. Your QA hire starts with a working suite instead of an empty repo, and you're covered in the meantime.
